## Context

The existing website no longer reflected Frédérique's artistic world. The challenge was to reorganize a large body of content: biography, music, albums, shows, press and archives.

## The solution

- Complete redesign of the interface and content structure.
- Stronger presentation of the artist's maritime world and personality.
- Integration of audio extracts, videos, albums, shows and press archives.
- Responsive adaptation for desktop and mobile.
- Focus on speed, readability and visual hierarchy.
