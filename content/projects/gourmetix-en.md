## The Challenge & Business Context

Restaurant owners, dark kitchens, and delivery franchises lose valuable orders every day on Uber Eats and Deliveroo due to amateur smartphone photos that look dull or poorly lit. However:
- Traditional on-site food photoshoots cost €800 to €1,500 and disrupt active kitchen service.
- 100% synthetic AI images mislead customers and violate consumer protection standards.

Gourmetix Studio required a premium, high-converting digital storefront that reassures restaurant professionals within seconds, emphasizes authentic dish enhancement without false advertising, and converts visitors directly into trial orders.

## The Technical & UX Solution

- **Astro & TypeScript architecture**: built as an ultra-fast static website with flawless Core Web Vitals and SEO performance.
- **Interactive Before / After slider**: custom pointer/touch comparison widget allowing visitors to visually experience the difference between raw phone snapshots and sales-ready visuals.
- **Zero-friction WhatsApp conversion funnel**: removed lengthy forms and account creation, enabling chefs and managers to send food photos directly via WhatsApp in under 2 minutes.
- **Full multilingual support (FR / EN / RU)**: complete localized journeys tailored to international culinary brands and franchises.
- **Local SEO & geo-targeting**: semantic anchors tailored for major French metropolitan areas (Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Bordeaux, Lille, Reims, etc.).
- **Accessibility & data privacy**: WCAG 2.2 compliant interactive elements, native accessible dialogs, and zero third-party trackers.
