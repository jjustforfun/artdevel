## The need

The practice needed a clear, reassuring and accessible website to present its care, fees, access information and booking process without adding unnecessary calls to the office.

## The solution

- Custom interface presenting the practice and its services.
- Practical information organized for quick reading on mobile.
- Doctolib integration to simplify online booking.
- Intelligent assistant limited to the practice's administrative and practical information.
- Responsive, accessible journey guiding each visitor to the right information.
