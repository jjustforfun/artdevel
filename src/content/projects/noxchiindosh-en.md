## The project

Noxchiindosh brings together Chechen poetry and folk songs in an editorial experience available in four languages.

## In-progress features

- Organization of a cultural and poetic text collection.
- Translations and multilingual navigation.
- Advanced text search.
- An interface designed for reading and discovery.
